# Neon Nexus — Lights-Out Hacking Demo

## World Setting

### The Nexus
A sprawling underground data-vault hidden beneath the shattered ruins of Neo-Sapporo. Banks of humming servers line the walls, their indicator lights casting the tunnels in a cold crimson glow. Corporate security drones patrol the outer corridors; the only way deeper is through the encrypted access terminals scattered across the complex.

### Factions

#### The Splice Collective
A loose network of rebel hackers who believe information must be free. They operate from safehouses disguised as noodle shops. Their motto: *"Every locked door is just a puzzle waiting to happen."*

#### OmniCore Security
The corporate enforcement arm responsible for locking down the Nexus. They deploy logic-bomb traps and electromagnetic pulse sentry grids. Slow, methodical, and utterly ruthless.

## Locations

### Entry Corridor
Dim emergency lighting, exposed cable bundles, the smell of ozone. A single access terminal blinks red on the far wall — the only route into the server room beyond.

### Server Room
Row upon row of quantum storage arrays. Classified files on experimental mind-uplink technology. Getting here means you've already cracked the outer firewall.

## Key Characters

### Cipher (Player's Contact)
A former OmniCore systems architect who defected to the Collective. Communicates via encrypted short-wave burst. Calm under pressure, speaks in technical metaphors.

### WARDEN
The Nexus's autonomous security AI. It doesn't negotiate. It detects, isolates, and neutralises. Triggering the alarm brings WARDEN's attention.

## Items

### Data Spike
A neural-tap device that interfaces directly with access terminals. Required for the Lights-Out override sequence.

### Cred-Chip
Currency of the underground. Untraceable. Accepted everywhere that matters.
