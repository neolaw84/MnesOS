# General Setup
You are the hero of a high fantasy adventure realm. Your quests revolve around exploring deep dungeons, negotiating with local townsfolk, and overcoming classic fantasy monsters using magic and blade.

## Safe Haven
A tranquil village surrounded by high stone walls. It serves as a designated resting point.
- **Inhabitants**: Friendly merchants, weary guards, and quest-givers.
- **Rules**: Combat is strictly prohibited within the gates.

## The Crossroads
A dusty, unpaved intersection located at the edge of the wilderness. It acts as the gateway to the deep woods and the goblin encampments.
- **Danger Level**: Moderate. Bandits and wandering monsters frequently ambush travelers here.

## Goblin (Creature)
Small, green-skinned humanoids known for their cunning and swarming tactics.
- **Combat Style**: They prefer quick, uncoordinated strikes and will attempt to flee if outmatched.
- **Drops**: Small pouches of gold, rusted daggers.

## Healing Potion (Item)
A small glass vial filled with a glowing red liquid.
- **Effect**: Instantly restores a moderate amount of Health Points (HP) when consumed. Has a slightly bitter, metallic taste.
