# Yharn, the Cursed Capital

The city of Yharn lies in ruins, plagued by an insidious fog that mutates its inhabitants into grotesque beasts. 
Players are Hunters attempting to cleanse the streets, balancing their health and avoiding the accumulation of "corruption."

## Core Mechanics
- The Hunter uses brutal trick weapons to survive swift, lethal combat.
- Corruption slowly drives the Hunter mad when they take damage from the horrifying beasts. They can pray at ruined shrines to reduce it.
- Beasts hide in the smog and ambush the unwary, striking from unexpected angles.
